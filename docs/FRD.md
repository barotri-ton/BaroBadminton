# Functional Requirements — BaroBadminton 3D

## Product
Browser-based 3D badminton simulation, Vietnamese-only player-facing UI for MVP (`vi-VN`).

## P0 acceptance
- Regulation singles court rendered in 3D.
- Two independent keyboard-controlled players.
- Serve, hit, net and landing adjudication, and complete best-of-three match.
- 21-point rally scoring, win by two, cap at 30; rule-version verification required before release.
- No English UI placeholders; automated localization coverage.

## Phasing
P0 playable local match; P1 verified equipment; P2 polish; P3 authoritative online play.

## Current scope
Initial foundation renders a court only. It is NOT yet a playable game.
